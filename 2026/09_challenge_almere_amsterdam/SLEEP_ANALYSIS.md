## Key Insights

- **Race-week taper produced the best sleep of the cycle**: score jumped from Aug's 68.0 average to 76 in the final week (Sep 7–11), duration hit a season-high 7h 54min, and the deficit shrank to just 10 minutes — the only week that nearly closed the gap to sleep need;
- **Bedtime discipline tracked sleep quality**: the latest bedtime of the cycle (1:47 AM) fell in the worst week (Aug 10–16, score 62); bedtime pulled back to 0:20–0:57 AM across the final three weeks, coinciding with the best scores of the cycle;
- **The deficit was persistent, not occasional**: every month averaged 1h 20min–1h 39min short of sleep need — this wasn't a few bad nights dragging the average down, it was the norm for 12 of the 13 weeks;
- **Slight improvement over the Bonn baseline**: 68.8 avg score and 6h 53min avg duration here versus 67.3 and 6h 24min for the Bonn cycle — a modest gain despite a shorter, more compressed 13-week build straight off the back of a race;
- **No catastrophic weeks this time**: unlike Bonn's single 48-score week (3h 42min sleep), the worst week here (62) was still within the "Fair" band — no outlier collapse, but also no week ever reached "Good".
